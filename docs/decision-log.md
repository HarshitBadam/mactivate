### 2026-07-24 — Deterministic palm-tap classifier implemented; replay fixtures expose firm-aftershock scoring correction
- **Decision:** `MactuationCore` now contains a deterministic batch `TapClassifier` implementing the documented H-TAP-PALM rule operation-for-operation with `scripts/analyze_imu.py`: 0.5 s centered moving-average detrending, ≥0.04 g onset threshold, 150 ms refractory, ≤1 s grouping, a uniform 1–3-member group limit, and a first-member-only tiered gate (comfort: positive ±25 ms Z impulse and lateral impulse <0.25 mg·s; firm: calibrated amplitude cut plus lateral/peak ≤5 (mg·s)/g and decay ≤150 ms). Its Codable calibration is explicitly versioned and stores firm-tier cuts by calibration side. The initial configuration includes only the measured left-side 0.25 g firm cut; the right side remains unqualified rather than inventing the missing per-side cut. Real-capture replay fixtures pin every group verdict for all palm takes and typing, trackpad, rest, desk, and both bump takes; identical capture plus configuration produces identical canonical output and digest.
- **Rationale:** Re-scoring the captures through an independent rule wrapper over `analyze_imu.py` reproduced the documented comfort/light results and zero false accepts exactly, including all four `baseline_bump_2` veto values. It also exposed a bookkeeping inconsistency in the earlier firm-take recall: 23/26 left and 24/32 right were obtained without applying the stated 1–3-member group limit to firm-tap aftershock groups. Applying the rule uniformly yields **21/26 left-firm and 23/32 right-firm**; the lost groups have 4–9 detected members from ring-down. The classifier follows the stated uniform rule and fails closed. Peak-scaled aftershock suppression remains the measured refinement needed to recover those groups before qualification.
- **Status:** Active. Implementation/replay parity is complete on the discovery captures. Still open before release: establish a right-side firm cut from repeated-session calibration, aftershock suppression and adaptive member thresholds, daemon-context access measurement, repeated-session recall/precision, and the deferred eight-hour false-positive run. No long-duration qualification result is claimed.

### 2026-07-24 — Exploratory probing ends: v1 scope is palm taps only; bump vetoes validated out-of-sample; desk taps and hand-near refuted and cut
- **Decision:** The probe loop's exploratory capture phase is **closed** and the v1 interaction scope is fixed to **H-TAP-PALM only** (single/double/triple palm-rest taps, either side, per-side calibrated). Three measurements settled it (see [Probe Results 2026-07-24](probe-results/2026-07-24-mac14-2-discovery.md)). **(1) Bump vetoes validated:** on the independent, differently-composed `baseline_bump_2` take the base tiered rule false-accepted 4 of 11 groups and the comfort-tier lateral veto (±25 ms |x|+|y| impulse ≥ 0.25 mg·s rejects) removed all four at zero measured palm recall cost (every comfort/light first member across six palm takes ≤ 0.216 mg·s, n=134; every ≥0.25 g firm first member passes the scaled lat/peak ≤ 5, decay ≤ 150 ms veto). Watch-items, not blockers: the weakest bump cleared the cut by only 6%, and the firm-tier veto is still backed by a single firm-tier bump event. **(2) H-TAP-DESK refuted and cut from v1:** the pre-stated spectral criterion (≥90% desk recall, ≤5% typing+bump acceptance on one spectral feature) failed by an order of magnitude — desk knock centroids (60 Hz [20..72]) sit inside the typing distribution (51–52 Hz [28..68]) with bump tails covering the rest; best achievable acceptance 76–79%. Every separator is now measured and exhausted. **(3) H-HAND-NEAR refuted for v1 and cut:** in the stationary-machine trial, walking/leaning shadows (median depth −48.5%, dwell 0.1–0.8 s, zero machine motion to gate on) dominate deliberate ~1 s hovers (median −32.7%, dwell ≤0.2 s at the 10 Hz ceiling) on every feature; no depth/slope/dwell rule separates them and a full-cover rule still fires on a light toggle. Combined with the dim-light (~1 lux floor) and dynamic-lighting refutations, no measured operating envelope remains. The unprivileged ALS acquisition path stays validated for possible future fusion work. Implementation of the palm-tap engine begins against the recorded captures.
- **Rationale:** The turn's instruction was kill-or-commit: validate the vetoes out-of-sample, test the last bounded separator for each open interaction, and cut anything that failed its criterion rather than requesting more captures. Both cuts are evidence-backed refutations on this machine, not deferrals; keeping them in scope would trade the product's zero-unintended-firing requirement for hope. Palm taps survived every adversarial take recorded against them (typing, trackpad, rest, two bump takes).
- **Status:** Active. Remaining pre-release qualification (not exploration): daemon-context privilege check, long-duration false-positive run, repeated-session recall/precision per Step 10, adaptive member thresholds and aftershock suppression as engine refinements, firm-tier veto margin monitoring.

### 2026-07-24 — Bump take breaks the palm rule's zero-false-accept record (lateral/decay vetoes measured); desk-tap gesture refuted-leaning; hand-near downgraded to lit-static-environment-only
- **Decision:** The three remaining false-positive/robustness takes are recorded (see [Probe Results 2026-07-24](probe-results/2026-07-24-mac14-2-discovery.md)). **H-TAP-PALM:** the `baseline_bump` take (39 s of desk bumps, machine shifts, lid adjustments, leaning) produces **3 false accepts in 8 groups** under the tiered rule — bump Z-impulse signs are near-random so the Z-gate does not reject them, and one 0.392 g bump passes the firm tier as a "triple". The same take measures a mitigation the engine direction now adopts pending validation: palm-tap first members carry ≤0.216 mg·s lateral (±25 ms X+Y) impulse while the false-accept bumps carry 0.337–8.424 mg·s, so a comfort-tier absolute lateral veto (~0.25 mg·s) plus a firm-tier scaled veto (lat/peak ≤ 5 (mg·s)/g and decay ≤ 150 ms) rejects all three false accepts with zero measured recall cost across every palm take (both sides, all intensities). These cuts are tuned on one bump take and require an independent bump take to validate. **H-TAP-DESK is downgraded to refuted-leaning:** 79 bump events swamp the desk band (0.015–0.037 g sits entirely inside the bump distribution) with no separating time-domain feature, and keyboard-idle gating is no defense since bumps occur while idle; only unexplored spectral features could rescue it. **H-HAND-NEAR is downgraded to conditionally supported:** dim-light hovering is refuted outright (sensor pinned at 1.0 lux, zero changes across 4–5 hovers × two takes, screen brightness irrelevant), and the lighting-change trial shows ALS-only detection cannot reject real lighting changes (4 hover-like firings in 45 s, one matching a deliberate hover on magnitude, slope, and dwell). The gesture is redefined as: deliberate hover, armed only above a minimum ambient baseline, gated by IMU/lid-motion vetoes.
- **Rationale:** These were the three takes the earlier verdicts flagged as critical. All three delivered kill-or-qualify evidence: bumps are the first measured source of tap false accepts (typing/trackpad/rest remain clean), the desk gesture's last non-spectral escape route (idle gating) is closed by data, and the hand-near gesture's operating envelope is now bounded by measurement rather than hope. Recording the veto thresholds as pending-validation keeps the same-take-tuning risk explicit.
- **Status:** Active. Next: independent `baseline_bump` take for out-of-sample veto validation; stationary-machine lighting-FP trial (shadows without carry/lid motion — the IMU cannot veto those); bright-room 10 Hz hover take for positive-class dwell/recovery margins; desk-vs-typing-vs-bump spectral analysis on existing captures; daemon-context privilege check; long-duration false-positive run.

### 2026-07-24 — H-TAP-REGION refuted with axis frame verified; per-side tap calibration becomes mandatory; product region mapping pivots to surface classes
- **Decision:** **H-TAP-REGION is refuted on the target machine** and the first product's palm-rest region mapping is redefined as **surface-class mapping (palm vs. desk), single region per surface**. Both confounds from the earlier refuted-leaning verdict were closed by new takes (see [Probe Results 2026-07-24](probe-results/2026-07-24-mac14-2-discovery.md)): a four-way marked tilt capture gravity-verified that the SPU's X axis is cleanly the physical lateral chassis axis (left/right edge lifts flip its sign; Y responds to front/back; ≤0.03 g cross-leakage), and the right-palm firm take completed the side × intensity matrix with the 150 ms X-impulse **positive in all four cells** — no sign flip exists to calibrate on. A weak tap-side-congruent ±25 ms X lean (60–75% per tap) is recorded but is unusable for per-region mappings. Secondly, **per-side calibration of the H-TAP-PALM tiers is now mandatory**: right-firm taps attenuate to 0.10–0.37 g (vs 0.27–0.55 g left) and half-break the Z-gate, dropping the left-calibrated rule to 75% recall (vs 88% left-firm). Housekeeping: the clean 800 Hz rest floor is established (0.0032 g max over 34 s, `baseline_rest_800`); `analyze_imu.py` now parses `labels.csv` marker spans, reports first-group-member features, and takes `--skip-s`; capture `20260724-055005-palm_right_single_800` is recorded as mislabeled (it is the right-palm firm take — stale shell-history label).
- **Rationale:** Evidence discipline demanded the axis-orientation check before a final region verdict — it turned out the analyzer's frame was fine and the physics simply lacks a lateral signature at this SPU placement; recording the residual weak lean keeps the refutation honest. The right-firm take was the last cell of the matrix and doubled as the discovery that both palm-tap discriminators weaken with distance from the SPU, which changes calibration from advisable to required.
- **Status:** Active. Remaining probe items: `baseline_bump` take, dim-light + lighting-change ALS trials, desk-vs-typing spectral work, adaptive member thresholds, aftershock suppression, daemon-context privilege check, long-duration false-positive run.

### 2026-07-24 — Desk taps detectable but not typing-separable; palm-rest region localization refuted-leaning on this hardware
- **Decision:** The right-palm and desk takes are recorded (see [Probe Results 2026-07-24](probe-results/2026-07-24-mac14-2-discovery.md)). **H-TAP-DESK splits:** knocks 10–15 cm away on a soft-wood table couple through the chassis clearly (0.015–0.037 g, 24/24 detected) and are trivially separable from palm taps (lower amplitude band + negative Z-impulse), but they sit inside the typing distribution on every measured feature — a desk-tap gesture is viable only with keyboard/trackpad-idle gating pending spectral-feature work and a `baseline_bump` take. **H-TAP-REGION is refuted-leaning:** no lateral impulse sign flip exists between left and right palm taps in either the ±25 ms or prior art's 150 ms window (150 ms X-impulse positive on both sides), and firm force does not rescue it on the left; final verdict awaits a right-firm take and an axis-orientation check. **H-TAP-PALM strengthens:** the Z-impulse gate holds on the right palm rest (25/28) with a measurably smaller margin than the left (median +0.039 vs +0.058 mg·s — SPU likely sits left of center; calibrate the gate per side), and the gate rejects desk knocks for free.
- **Rationale:** These were the two untested hypotheses gating the product's region-mapping promise. The desk and region evidence both point the same way: the SPU sees *whether* the chassis was excited and from which mechanical path class (palm deck vs. key/trackpad/desk), not *where* along the deck — the product's per-region mapping needs a rethink toward surface-class mapping (palm vs. desk) unless the pending takes overturn this.
- **Status:** Active. Pending: right-palm firm take, axis-orientation check, clean 800 Hz rest baseline, `baseline_bump` take.

### 2026-07-24 — Both flagship interactions validated on target hardware; tap detection direction set to Z-impulse gating at ~800 Hz
- **Decision:** Probe Steps 5–6, 8, and most of 9 are recorded as executed with labelled operator takes on the target machine (see [Probe Results 2026-07-24](probe-results/2026-07-24-mac14-2-discovery.md)). **H-HAND-NEAR is supported** (deliberate hover = −90% lux, zero steady-light drift, 5 Hz default cadence raisable to a measured 10 Hz ceiling via unprivileged `ReportInterval` write; hover ~0.2–0.3 s latency; quick swipes unreliable — the gesture is a hover, not a swipe). **H-TAP-PALM is supported across the intensity range** via a locally discovered discriminator: palm-rest taps produce a positive Z-axis impulse at the SPU while typing and trackpad clicks produce a negative one. The engine's tap-detector direction is set to a tiered design at ~800 Hz (also confirmed available unprivileged): onset events on detrended magnitude, timing-window grouping, first-member Z-impulse gate for light/comfortable taps, plus a high-amplitude tier (≥~0.25 g) for firm taps. Scored on this session's data: 88–100% group recall on singles/doubles/triples, zero false accepts across all 800 Hz typing/trackpad/rest baselines.
- **Rationale:** Step 8's separability question had a decisive empirical answer in the labelled takes; amplitude-only separation failed for comfortable-force taps (typing reaches 0.238 g vs. comfort taps ≤0.15 g), so the validated Z-impulse feature is what makes the product's gentle-tap interaction viable at all. 800 Hz is required — the impulse estimate produced a typing false-accept at 100 Hz.
- **Status:** Active. Open before productization: adaptive member thresholds for multi-tap counting, aftershock suppression for firm hits, right-palm/desk takes (H-TAP-REGION, H-TAP-DESK untested), dim-light and lighting-change ALS trials, daemon-context privilege check, and the long-duration false-positive qualification run.

### 2026-07-24 — SPU IMU data flow validated unprivileged at ~100 Hz; root assumption refuted; architecture lean must be revisited
- **Decision:** Probe Steps 3–4 are recorded as locally validated via the new `MactuationProbe` tool: 22-byte reports, int32-LE offsets 6/10/14 ÷ 65536 decode confirmed against gravity (z ≈ −1.0007 g at rest), ~99.7 Hz effective delivery, wake sequence required (state properties absent until set; saved/restored on exit). **HID open, wake writes, and report delivery all succeeded as euid 501 — no root, no TCC prompt — independently reproduced twice.** `discover` now measures privileges with a real open-probe instead of assuming prior art. Consequently, [Architecture Options](architecture-options.md) assumption 1 ("SPU IMU requires root") is refuted for interactive user sessions on this machine, and the preliminary lean toward a privileged helper is **under review**: the entire preferred sensor set (IMU + ALS registry poll) currently needs no elevation. The helper decision now hinges on daemon/launchd-context behavior and lifecycle evidence (sleep/wake, relaunch), not on root.
- **Rationale:** The probe plan's own instruction: "If Step 4 of the probe shows the IMU is readable without root … this recommendation should be revisited and the change logged." All three prior-art implementations required sudo on earlier macOS versions; either macOS 26 relaxed HID access for console users or the interactive-user context was always sufficient and untested. CoreMotion remains refuted (`accelerometerAvailable = false`), so IOKit HID stays the only working path.
- **Status:** Active. Verify daemon-context access and lifecycle behavior before finalizing architecture; keep the engine boundary transport-agnostic regardless.

### 2026-07-21 — Hardware-independent Mactuation Engine core implemented before the probe
- **Decision:** Implementation starts with `MactuationCore`, a Swift package containing only the parts of the engine that do not depend on unverified hardware facts: typed sensor samples and paths, the capability state model (all real paths default to `unknown`), the capture-session format from the Local Probe Plan Step 7 (per-path CSV streams, `labels.csv`, `session.json`), a deterministic replay source, a stream digest for the deterministic-replay quality gate, and a seeded mock source. Real acquisition adapters (SPU HID, DisplayServices ALS, consented fallbacks) remain probe deliverables and are not written off-Mac.
- **Rationale:** The recorder/replay/labelled-data workflow is required regardless of which sensor paths survive the probe, and building it first means the probe tool lands on tested infrastructure. Writing IOKit adapters without the target machine would encode unvalidated report layouts, wake sequences, and privilege assumptions. The package is Foundation-only so its tests also run off-Mac.
- **Status:** Active. The first hardware code remains the probe tool per the Local Probe Plan handoff.

### 2026-07-21 — Determinism, measured reliability, privacy clarity, and polished UX are release requirements
- **Decision:** Identical recorded input plus identical versioned configuration must classify identically; accepted events dispatch exactly once; ambiguous input fails closed. Product qualification requires repeated labelled sessions, lifecycle/interruption tests, and a long representative false-positive run. Camera/microphone fallbacks are explicit opt-ins with persistent truthful status, prompt device release, and no raw-media retention without separate consent. Accessibility and responsive, non-blocking UI are part of acceptance.
- **Rationale:** Physical sensors are noisy and privacy-sensitive actions can be disruptive. “Works in a demo” is insufficient for a background utility that runs shortcuts or keeps a macOS privacy indicator active.
- **Status:** Active. Initial numerical gates are documented in [Gesture Hypotheses](research/gesture-hypotheses.md) and may be tightened as target-hardware evidence accumulates.

### 2026-07-21 — First product scope narrowed to hand-near opening and simple taps
- **Decision:** The primary experience is a hand near the camera/notch area opening a notch-attached workspace occupying approximately 60% of the screen. That workspace maps calibrated palm-rest and nearby-table regions' single, double, and triple taps to actions. The menu-bar app is secondary. Cross-region sequences, arbitrary rhythms, lid gestures, and movement/orientation gestures are out of scope.
- **Rationale:** A narrow interaction vocabulary keeps the first product centered on its distinctive physical interaction and gives the large surface one clear job: spatial tap-region mapping.
- **Sensor preference:** Prefer ambient-light sensing over camera input for hand-near detection and accelerometer sensing over microphone input for taps. Camera and microphone remain acceptable fallbacks because reliability is unvalidated, but should be avoided when possible due to privacy sensitivity and macOS's green/orange privacy indicators.
- **Status:** Active. The sensor implementations remain hypotheses subject to the local probe; the product boundary is a deliberate choice.

### 2026-07-21 — Current hardware assumptions are hypotheses, not facts
- **Decision:** Every claim about the target MacBook's sensors — that the SPU accelerometer/gyroscope is present and root-gated, that ALS and lid angle are readable, HID usages/offsets/scales, sample rates, units, and model/macOS compatibility — is recorded as **source-backed prior art or hypothesis**, never as a locally validated fact, until the [Local Probe Plan](local-probe-plan.md) confirms it on the actual hardware.
- **Rationale:** This work was initialized in a non-macOS cloud environment with no access to the target MacBook, so no hardware behavior could be observed. Prior art is consistent and credible but is other people's reports on other machines; several details already conflict across sources (e.g. lid-angle units, exact model compatibility).
- **Status:** Active. To be revised entry-by-entry as probe results arrive.

### 2026-07-21 — Architecture remains open until local sensor evidence exists
- **Decision:** No final architecture is selected. The [Architecture Options](architecture-options.md) doc states only a *preliminary* lean (self-contained Mactuation Engine with a clean boundary; start in-process/`sudo` for the probe phase; a small privileged helper as the likely product shape). The binding choice is deferred until the probe establishes real privilege requirements and achievable rate/latency.
- **Rationale:** The privilege domain of the sensors (root) and of the actions (TCC grants), the achievable IPC throughput, and even which sensors need root are all unmeasured. Committing to an architecture now would encode unvalidated assumptions.
- **Status:** Active. Revisit after Probe Step 4 (privileges) and any IPC rate measurement.
